Quiet reminders

Loading reminder status...

One notification at most every 7 elapsed days, only when something is eligible, between 09:00 and 18:00 local time. First opportunity is after one week. Later waits at least 14 elapsed days, then shares future reminder slots. These are initial product policies, not measured optima.

Notifications contain no bookmark titles or URLs. Chrome must be able to run; sleep, Chrome being quit, Focus/Do Not Disturb and notification settings can delay or suppress them. No exact delivery time or proof you saw a reminder is claimed. Turning reminders off keeps decisions and recovery copies.

What happens to a bookmark?

Remove bookmark deletes one Chrome bookmark after confirmation and saves a local recovery copy. Chrome may sync removal or restoration to other devices. Keep as reference pauses reviews and reminders for 365 elapsed days from your decision, including existing kept records. Choosing Keep again starts another year. Stop suggesting has no expiry and leaves the bookmark in Chrome. Undo or changing the bookmark’s URL makes it eligible again; renaming or moving it does not reset a decision. Later suppresses the bookmark for at least 14 days, including in Review more. Choosing Later again restarts that wait. Eligibility does not guarantee immediate selection or notification. An ignored reminder does not resolve anything or escalate; that item waits at least 14 days before another offer. Review decisions lets you undo local choices or restore removed bookmarks as new bookmarks. Recovery cannot restore original dates or IDs.

Why this bookmark?

We start with older recorded activity, mixing in bookmarks with unknown dates. Reminders rotate between due Later items and other eligible bookmarks, including references whose year has elapsed. Dates are clues, not proof of whether you read something or still care about it. We do not access browser history.

Coming back

Enable optional quiet reminders once, or use the Backburner toolbar button for manual review. Declining notification permission leaves manual review available. An unfinished session resumes automatically; a reminder never silently replaces it. Reminder reviews consider one bookmark and do not change your manual session size. There are no streaks or automatic website visits.

Data & permissions

Chrome’s bookmarks permission allows reading and changing bookmarks. Backburner reads them, removes only individually confirmed bookmarks, and creates bookmarks when you explicitly restore. Storage saves decisions, recovery and scheduling on this device. Alarms wake the local reminder scheduler; optional notification permission lets it display reminders. No account, remote code, page scanning, analytics service, or data transmission.

We store bookmark IDs, URL fingerprints to recognize changed bookmarks, decisions, counts, and removal recovery copies. Separate reminder records store enablement, attempt times, rotation and pending bookmark IDs/fingerprints, not titles or URLs. They are operational scheduling state, not feedback or impression analytics. Recovery copies include titles, URLs, and original location information. They are retained until restored or explicitly forgotten. Fingerprints are not anonymization. Local records do not sync, and uninstalling removes them. Chrome’s own bookmark sync is independent.

Opening a bookmark visits that website normally. The site’s own privacy practices apply.
